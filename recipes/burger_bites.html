Smash Burger Bowl with Southern-Style Hash Browns

Home

These smash burger bowls have everything you love about a smash burger, minus the bun. Served on a bed of Southern-style hash browns with lettuce, tomato, and a quick burger sauce, they’re packed with flavor.

Ingredients

  • Avocado oil
  • 2 Cups of Frozen hash brown pototes
  • 1 pound of fresh beef
  • 4 slices of american cheese
  • 1 roma tomato
  • 4 cruchy lettuces
  • 6 green onions
  • burger sauce

Steps

  1. Add enough avocado oil to cover the bottom of a heavy skillet; heat over medium heat. Cook potatoes on all sides until golden brown and crispy, about 12 minutes. Remove and add to a paper towel-lined plate. Set aside and keep warm.
  2. For burgers, season meatballs with salt and pepper. Bring either the same skillet you cooked the potatoes in or another griddle to medium-high heat. Add meatballs. Using the back of a heavy spatula, smash each meatball into a patty, flipping regularly until well browned, about 5 minutes. You may have to work in batches
  3. Add a quartered slice of cheese to each patty, then briefly cover the skillet with a lid to melt the cheese. Set cooked patties aside when ready. Cover to keep warm while you cook the remaining patties.
  4. To assemble bowls, divide potatoes into 4 individual serving bowls. Divide lettuce and green onion evenly among the bowls. Top with tomato slices and burger patties. Drizzle with sauce.
